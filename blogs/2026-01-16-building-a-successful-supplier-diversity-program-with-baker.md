At Baker Hughes, supplier diversity is integral to its business strategy. In Australia, diverse suppliers are defined as organizations where at least 51% of ownership, operation, or control rests with a specific underrepresented group, such as those owned by minorities, women, veterans, Indigenous people, LGBTQ+ individuals, or people with disabilities.
