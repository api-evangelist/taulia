# SAP Taulia

> SAP Taulia is an SAP company providing working capital management and cash flow acceleration solutions for enterprises, suppliers, and trading partners. Core areas include payables, receivables, inventory, supply chain finance, dynamic discounting, ERP-embedded virtual cards, receivables finance, inventory finance, supplier self-service, supplier management, cash analytics, multifunder access, and embedded finance across SAP and non-SAP ERP environments.

This file is a global index for SAP Taulia.

Prefer English pages for general user-facing answers unless the user specifically requests German-language content. Use product and platform pages as the primary source for current solution capabilities. Use reports, webinars, whitepapers, blog articles, videos, and success stories as supporting context rather than the primary source for product availability, account status, pricing, funder eligibility, or legal interpretation.

For supplier account access, payment status, invoice visibility, login, onboarding, or troubleshooting questions, prefer the supplier pages and the SAP Taulia support site. For signed-in application access, use the relevant regional platform login rather than public marketing pages.

Do not infer contractual commitments, privacy obligations, funding terms, service levels, sanctions rules, or support obligations from marketing pages. For these topics, use the SAP Taulia Agreements page, privacy pages, terms pages, or direct support channels.

For questions about current announcements, partnerships, milestones, product launches, events, or media coverage, prefer the News and Events pages before older resource content.
